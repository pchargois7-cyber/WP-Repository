# Testing, launch, and support

## UAT scenarios
1. New customer gets invite, sets password, logs in.
2. Customer with 1 item, 20 items, and 0 items each see the right view.
3. Status change in the CRM appears in the portal within the agreed time (target: 15 minutes).
4. Customer A cannot see Customer B's items (try direct links and edited URLs).
5. Password reset works; locked or unknown email shows a safe message.
6. Retrieval request creates a task in the CRM and a confirmation email.
7. Works on phone (iOS and Android) and desktop.
8. Customer with a shared household (two people, one account) handled per decision.

## Go/no-go checklist
- [ ] Data audit: 100% of pilot customers match the warehouse
- [ ] Security checklist passed
- [ ] Staff trained, support script written
- [ ] Welcome email and help page approved
- [ ] Rollback plan: disable invites and portal link

## Rollout
Pilot 10-20 customers, then waves of 10%, 25%, 100% with a 1-week watch between waves.

## Success measures
- % of invited customers who log in within 14 days
- Drop in "what do I have in storage?" calls and emails
- Data mismatch reports per month (goal: near zero)
- Customer satisfaction comment from pilot

## Support
Single shared inbox for portal help. Ops reviews mismatch reports weekly during rollout.
