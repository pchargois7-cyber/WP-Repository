**Subject:** CSMS Customer Storage Portal: plan, your role, and how we'll work together

Hi team,

We're building a secure online portal where Curtis Specialized Moving & Storage customers can log in and see what they have in storage. It will be built on GoHighLevel and connected to our CRM, so customers get answers without calling the office, and staff get time back.

**How it will work**
Think of the portal as a front window onto our warehouse. Customers can look at their own items, but only our team can change what's on the shelves. The CRM stays the single source of truth.

**What customers will be able to do**
- Log in securely and see only their own items, locations and status
- Request a retrieval or ask a question, which lands as a task for our team
- Receive notices when an item's status changes

**Our process (8 phases, each ending with a sign-off)**
0. Discovery: confirm systems and data
1. Design: pick the build approach and screens
2. Data foundation: get inventory records clean and linked to customers
3. Build
4. Testing with staff and a few friendly customers
5. Pilot launch
6. Full rollout
7. Ongoing operation and improvements
